Dual Screen DIY CyberDeck

Posted by Colin on Wed, Jul 30, 2025

A cyberdeck is a custom-built, portable computer, often with a cyberpunk aesthetic, designed for accessing and interacting with cyberspace. In science fiction, they are used by hackers to connect to digital worlds, often for activities like hacking, data theft, or controlling machines. Real-world cyberdecks are typically built around single-board computers and designed to evoke this cyberpunk style and scratch an itch for the maker who designs it.

There are plenty of “cyberdeck” builds out there but this latest one from Sector 07 has really caught my eye. It is a Dual Screen DIY Cyberdeck built around the Raspberry Pi single board computer (opens in a new tab) featuring twin rotating touchscreens.

This one has it all.

  • Stunning retro-futuristic look

  • Dual rotating touch screens (opens in a new tab)

  • Snap-fit 3D printed enclosure

  • Access to GPIO for prototyping

  • Integrated keyboard

  • Open source files and parts so you can build your own

  • Extensive documentation (opens in a new tab)

The attention to detail that Sector 07 have given to this device is amazing. It looks like a finished product and the aesthetic is super cool.

Take a break to watch the video below, it really is worth your time and if you decide to build one yourself or have other cyberdecks you think we should know about let us know in the comments.
